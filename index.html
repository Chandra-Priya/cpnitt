Research

Cryptography and Network Security

Authentication protocols with ring signatures, and hardware-assisted image encryption for access control in healthcare settings.

Distributed Systems Security

Blockchain-based trust, resilient knowledge bases, and traceable, access-controlled data aggregation.

IoT Data Security and Privacy

Protecting data across IoT and cloud pipelines, including metadata aggregation and vulnerability detection in domains such as maritime logistics.

Consensus Protocol Design

Modelling and evaluating consortium blockchain consensus, including diversity-mining proof of work and bi-layered consensus.

Research Group Focus

Decentralized AI and Trust Systems (DATS) Lab

The DATS Lab actively drives advanced research initiatives spanning Ph.D. investigations, M.Tech scholarly projects, and focused short-term/long-term research internships. Current focus areas include fault-tolerant decentralized asynchronous federated learning, graph-enhanced LLVM compiler optimizations, dynamic federated learning with model versioning and client unlearning, and decentralized AI solutions.
